# Test images

The repository ships a full 2D test suite in
[`orientationj-test-images/`](https://github.com/Biomedical-Imaging-Group/OrientationJ/tree/master/orientationj-test-images):
**16 grayscale images** — seven synthetic ones with a known ground truth, eight
real micrographs and one artificial fiber phantom — each with a binary **mask**
of the meaningful structures, and a **gallery** showing, for every image:
original, mask, orientation, coherency, energy, color survey, orientation
distribution inside the mask, and vector field. Every image can be downloaded
individually from the folder.

## Synthetic, with ground truth

| image | truth |
|---|---|
| `synthetic_chirp_1024` | radial chirp — exact tangential orientation, frequency sweep |
| `synthetic_rings_dither_512` | thin dithered rings — exact tangential orientation |
| `synthetic_wave_512` | fringes at exactly +60° and −30°, two scales at once |
| `synthetic_nematic_512` | nematic-like texture with a dominant direction |
| `synthetic_filaments_512` | random curved filaments |
| `synthetic_spiral_512` | spiral |
| `synthetic_noise_512` | isotropic noise — any measured anisotropy is bias |

The chirp is the fastest installation check: run **OrientationJ Dominant Direction**
or **Analysis** on it and the orientation must follow the local stripe direction at
every radius.

## Real micrographs

Collagen fibers, a fluorescence cell (actin), a wood section (growth rings), a
fibronectin network, fibrous tissue, SEM nanofibers, a polymer slice, and the
montage of the Fiji Directionality documentation — plus one artificial fiber
phantom (drawn, not generated by the synthetic-image notebook).

Example — the collagen panel from the gallery:

![Collagen gallery panel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Biomedical-Imaging-Group/OrientationJ/master/orientationj-test-images/gallery/collagen.png)

The complete gallery, with one such panel per image, is in the
[folder README](https://github.com/Biomedical-Imaging-Group/OrientationJ/tree/master/orientationj-test-images#gallery).
The panels are generated by the
[Python port](https://github.com/Biomedical-Imaging-Group/OrientationJ/tree/master/orientationj-python-port)
with the plugin defaults (cubic-spline gradient, σ = 1).

## External datasets with ground truth

For validation beyond a smoke test, published datasets are also useful:

- **Hotaling et al. (2015), *Data in Brief*** — synthetic fiber images drawn at specified
  angles, SEM images of steel wire with known diameters, electrospun nanofiber SEM images,
  their segmentations, and reference orientation histograms.
  [doi:10.1016/j.dib.2015.07.012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2015.07.012)
- **Arafat et al. (2025), *PLOS ONE*** — four extracellular-matrix datasets with manually
  delineated ground truth, used to benchmark seven fiber-tracing algorithms.
  [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0320006](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0320006)
