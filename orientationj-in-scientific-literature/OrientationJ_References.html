OrientationJ is a series of ImageJ/Fiji plugins written by Daniel Sage at the Biomedical Imaging Group (BIG), EPFL, Switzerland. It characterizes the local orientation and isotropy (coherency) of structures in an image by evaluating the gradient structure tensor in a local neighborhood, and offers color-coded orientation maps, orientation distributions (histograms), vector fields, dominant-direction and ROI measurements, and Harris-corner keypoint detection. This document lists peer-reviewed publications (2013–2026) that used OrientationJ, together with the sentence from each paper describing how the plugin was used, followed by an appendix of primary references, official software pages, community discussions and related tools.
